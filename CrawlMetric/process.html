03 / OUR PROCESS

Less guesswork.
More direction.

A working outline of how we’ll approach
your website, together.

  1. 01 — LISTEN

    Start with your business.

    Understand your goals, your customers, and what your website needs to help them do.

  2. 02 — INVESTIGATE

    Look beneath the surface.

    Review the structure, content, and technical foundations that shape the experience.

  3. 03 — PRIORITIZE

    Make the next step clear.

    Translate findings into a focused plan, with context and a sensible order of work.

  4. 04 — REFINE

    Improve with intention.

    Agree on changes, put them into practice, and review what still needs attention.
