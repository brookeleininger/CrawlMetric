PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE / WEB SEO INTERN

Sam M. Walton
College of Business

During my May–August internship as a Web SEO Intern, I supported the Sam M. Walton College of Business website through technical SEO audits, accessibility improvements, and hands-on content optimization. Across 20+ website crawls, I repeatedly reviewed accessibility, search discoverability, broken links, metadata, indexability, and other technical issues. I fixed broken links, added and refined metadata, and updated images with meaningful alt text to improve search visibility, usability, and the overall website experience. The internship taught me how thoughtful technical work can create measurable impact and helped me discover that SEO, accessibility, and web optimization are the areas I want to pursue in my career.
