02 / ACCESSIBILITY BRIEF

Title II is changing the standard for public-sector websites.

The Department of Justice’s web accessibility rule gives state and local governments a specific technical standard for web content and mobile apps.

TECHNICAL STANDARD WCAG 2.1 LEVEL AA

Web content and mobile apps provided by covered state and local government entities generally need to meet this standard.

POPULATION 50,000+APR 262027

Current compliance deadline for larger state and local government entities.

UNDER 50,000 + SPECIAL DISTRICTSAPR 262028

Current compliance deadline for smaller entities and special district governments.

Where a CrawlMetric review can help: identify potential barriers, document affected pages, prioritize remediation, and re-test improvements. Automated findings still require human review.
