WHAT THE CRAWL CAN REVEAL

Find the barriers.
Focus the fixes.

I use crawl data and focused review to find website issues, explain their impact, and decide what to fix first.

01 / LINKS & RESPONSES

Links that lead somewhere.

Identify 4XX and 5XX responses, redirect chains, loops, and the pages where broken links originate.

02 / METADATA & HEADINGS

Clear page titles & headings.

Review page titles, meta descriptions, H1s, and H2s for missing, duplicated, or poorly sized content.

03 / IMAGES

Images with the right details.

Locate images with missing alt attributes or alt text and flag image files that may need optimization.

04 / INDEXABILITY

Pages search can discover.

Review robots directives, noindex signals, canonicals, and other instructions that affect how pages are discovered.

05 / CONTENT QUALITY

Content worth finding.

Surface duplicate and near-duplicate pages, low-content pages, and optional spelling or grammar findings.

06 / ACCESSIBILITY

Barriers that need attention.

Use automated accessibility validation to flag potential WCAG-related issues, then identify where human review is still required.
